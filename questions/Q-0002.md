# Q-0002 — How should the 280-character limit of a delivery note count?

## Question

The issue LIN-GTB-2 limits the customer's delivery note to 280 characters
because the warehouse label printer cuts off after that. For now we have decided
(DEC-0001, DEC-0002) that the limit counts characters (so "č" counts as one),
after leading and trailing spaces are removed; that a line break inside the
note is kept and counts as one character; and that an order with a longer note
is refused rather than cut short. Does that match how the label printer counts,
and may a note contain line breaks?

## Pitanje

Zadatak LIN-GTB-2 ograničava napomenu kupca za dostavu na 280 znakova jer pisač
naljepnica u skladištu reže tekst nakon toga. Zasad smo odlučili (DEC-0001,
DEC-0002) da se broje znakovi (tako da se „č” broji kao jedan), nakon što se
uklone razmaci na početku i kraju; da se prijelaz u novi red unutar napomene
zadržava i broji kao jedan znak; te da se narudžba s duljom napomenom odbija,
a ne skraćuje. Odgovara li to načinu na koji pisač naljepnica broji znakove i
smije li napomena sadržavati prijelaze u novi red?

## Why it matters

If the printer counts bytes rather than characters, or prints a line break
differently, a note within our limit could still be cut off on the label.

## Options we see

- Characters, line breaks allowed (the current decision).
- Characters, line breaks not allowed (replaced by spaces, or refused).
- Bytes, so notes with "č", "ć" or "ž" get a lower limit.

## Suggested answer

A guess: characters, with line breaks allowed.
