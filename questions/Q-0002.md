# Q-0002 — Which orders can a customer cancel?

## Question

Cancel moves from the My orders list into the order screen (LIN-GTB-3). Which
orders should offer it? For now we show it for placed orders only, not for
shipped or cancelled ones (DEC-0004). Is that right?

## Pitanje

Otkazivanje se iz popisa Moje narudžbe premješta na zaslon narudžbe
(LIN-GTB-3). Za koje narudžbe ga treba ponuditi? Zasad ga prikazujemo samo za
zaprimljene (placed) narudžbe, a ne za poslane ili otkazane (DEC-0004). Je li to
ispravno?

## Why it matters

FR-0003 shows or hides the Cancel button by the order's status. A wrong rule
lets customers cancel orders already on their way, or keeps them from
cancelling ones they still could.

## Options we see

- Placed orders only (current default, DEC-0004).
- The same orders the list offers Cancel for today, whatever that rule is.
- Placed orders, and shipped ones until some later step.

## Suggested answer

A guess: placed orders only, since a shipped order can't be stopped.
