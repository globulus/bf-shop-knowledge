# Q-0001 — How does the warehouse see an order's delivery note?

## Question

The issue LIN-GTB-2 says the customer's delivery note is something "that the
warehouse sees", and that the warehouse label printer cuts off after 280
characters. Shopfront has no warehouse screen. For now we have decided
(DEC-0007) that the warehouse sees the note through the order as orders-service
returns it, and that nothing else is built for the warehouse. How does the
warehouse read orders today, and does it need anything more to see the note,
for example the note printed on the label, or a warehouse view of orders?

## Pitanje

Zadatak LIN-GTB-2 kaže da je napomena kupca za dostavu nešto što „skladište
vidi”, te da pisač naljepnica u skladištu reže tekst nakon 280 znakova.
Shopfront nema zaslon za skladište. Zasad smo odlučili (DEC-0007) da skladište
vidi napomenu kroz narudžbu kakvu vraća orders-service i da se za skladište
ništa drugo ne izrađuje. Kako skladište danas čita narudžbe i treba li mu još
nešto da bi vidjelo napomenu, npr. napomenu otisnutu na naljepnici ili prikaz
narudžbi za skladište?

## Why it matters

If the warehouse reads orders some other way (an export, a print-out, a label
the system prints), the note won't reach it until that is changed too, and
customers will keep emailing support.

## Options we see

- The warehouse reads orders from orders-service (or its database) as it is;
  nothing more is needed (the current decision).
- The note must be printed on the warehouse label: a new requirement.
- The warehouse needs its own view of orders with the note: a new requirement.

## Suggested answer

A guess: the warehouse already reads orders from orders-service and nothing
more is needed for now.
