# Q-0001 — The Figma frames taken in with LIN-GTB-3 are of another product

## Question

Linear issue GTB-3 ("New 'My orders' screen in the app") links its design at
Figma node 4687-11685 and says to take spacing, type and the empty-state
illustration (on the file's "Assets" page) from it. The eight frames Globuli
exported with it (`inputs/attachments/LIN-GTB-3/`) show none of that: they are
section titles ("i", "Local components"), a component sheet of people,
messages, navigation and video calls, a "Today" calendar screen ("Active Collab
Card") and a "Campus" chat list ("Virtual rooms"). Only the first 8 frames were
exported (`intake.figma.max_frames`). Can you take in the frames that show My
orders, the order screen and the empty-state illustration (for example by
raising the frame limit, linking those frames in the issue, or attaching the
exports to a note)?

## Pitanje

Linear zadatak GTB-3 („Novi zaslon 'Moje narudžbe' u aplikaciji") povezuje
dizajn na Figma čvoru 4687-11685 i kaže da se razmaci, tipografija i ilustracija
praznog stanja (na stranici „Assets") uzmu iz njega. Osam okvira koje je Globuli
izvezao (`inputs/attachments/LIN-GTB-3/`) ne prikazuje ništa od toga: to su
naslovi sekcija, list komponenti (osobe, poruke, navigacija, video pozivi),
zaslon kalendara „Today" i popis razgovora „Campus". Izvezeno je samo prvih 8
okvira. Možete li unijeti okvire koji prikazuju Moje narudžbe, zaslon narudžbe i
ilustraciju praznog stanja (npr. većim ograničenjem broja okvira, poveznicama na
te okvire u zadatku ili izvozima priloženima bilješci)?

## Why it matters

FR-0005 (spacing and type per Figma) has no testable criterion without the
frames, and FR-0004 (empty state) can't be built or checked without the
illustration. FR-0001 to FR-0003 are written from the issue's text and don't
wait on this.

## Options we see

- Raise `intake.figma.max_frames` or point the issue's Figma link at the My
  orders frame, so the intake exports the right frames.
- Attach the exported frames and the illustration to a note in `inputs/`.
- Drop the Figma fidelity part and build from the text alone (FR-0005 would be
  rejected by the maintainer, FR-0004 would need another illustration).
