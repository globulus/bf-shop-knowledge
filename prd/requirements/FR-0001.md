# FR-0001 — My orders list as order cards grouped by month

## User story

As a customer using the mobile app, I want my orders shown as cards with their
date, status and total, grouped by month, so that I can see at a glance what I
ordered when and where each order stands.

## Context

The app's My orders screen today lists each order as its number, item count and
total. The client's Linear issue replaces that with cards grouped by month:

> "The current My orders list is just order number, item count and total. In the
> new design:
> * Each order is a card with the date, the status as a coloured chip (placed =
>   amber, shipped = green, cancelled = grey) and the total.
> * Orders are grouped by month with a sticky month header." — LIN-GTB-3
>
> *(The issue is written in English.)*

Decided by default, the client may still change them:

- **DEC-0001**: the card shows the date, the status chip and the total, and no
  longer the order number or item count; the order number moves to the order
  screen (FR-0002). Q-0003 asks the client to confirm.
- **DEC-0002**: orders and month groups run newest first; an order belongs to
  the calendar month of its order date in the device's time zone; dates and
  month headers follow the app's locale.

The issue defers spacing and type to its Figma file; that part is FR-0005.
Moving Cancel off the list is FR-0003; the empty list is FR-0004.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1 — Card content

- **Given** a customer with at least one order
- **When** they open My orders in the app
- **Then** each order is shown as one card with the order's date, its status as
  a chip and its total, and the card shows neither the order number nor the
  item count (DEC-0001)

### AC-2 — Status chip colours

- **Given** orders with the statuses placed, shipped and cancelled
- **When** the customer opens My orders
- **Then** the chip of a placed order is amber, of a shipped order green, and of
  a cancelled order grey, each with the status's name as localized text

### AC-3 — Grouping by month

- **Given** orders dated in more than one calendar month
- **When** the customer opens My orders
- **Then** the orders are grouped under one header per month, showing the month
  and year, with the newest month first and the newest order first within a
  month (DEC-0002)

### AC-4 — Sticky month header

- **Given** a month group longer than the screen
- **When** the customer scrolls through it
- **Then** that month's header stays pinned at the top of the list until the
  next month's header reaches it and takes its place

### AC-5 — Card opens the order

- **Given** the My orders list
- **When** the customer taps a card
- **Then** the app opens that order's screen (FR-0002)

## Examples

Grouping and order, with made-up orders (DEC-0002):

| Order date (device time) | Status | Shown under | Position |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-14 | placed | October 2026 | 1st group, 1st card |
| 2026-10-02 | shipped | October 2026 | 1st group, 2nd card |
| 2026-09-30 23:40 | cancelled | September 2026 | 2nd group, 1st card |
| 2026-08-05 | shipped | August 2026 | 3rd group, 1st card |
