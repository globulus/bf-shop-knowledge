# FR-0004 — My orders empty state with the Figma illustration

## User story

As a customer using the mobile app who has no orders yet, I want My orders to
tell me so clearly, so that an empty screen doesn't look broken.

## Context

> "Empty state uses the illustration from the Figma file (it's exported in the
> "Assets" page)." — LIN-GTB-3

The illustration was not among the frames taken in with the issue: the eight
exported frames of LIN-GTB-3 ("i", "Local components", "Active Collab Card",
"Virtual rooms") show screens of an unrelated product (a calendar, a "Campus"
chat list, video-call components), not My orders and not an Assets page, and
the issue's Figma link leads to more frames than were exported. Without the
illustration the empty state can't be built or checked against the design, so
this requirement waits on Q-0001 (maintainer).

Decided by default (DEC-0006), the client may still change it: besides the
illustration, the empty state shows a short localized message saying there are
no orders yet.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1 — Empty state

- **Given** a customer with no orders
- **When** they open My orders
- **Then** the screen shows the empty-state illustration from the Figma file's
  "Assets" page and a localized message that there are no orders yet
  (DEC-0006), and no month headers or cards

### AC-2 — Not shown with orders

- **Given** a customer with at least one order
- **When** they open My orders
- **Then** the empty state is not shown

## Out of scope

- Dark-mode variant of the illustration (DEC-0005).
