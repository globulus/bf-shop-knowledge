# FR-0003 — Cancel an order from the order screen

## User story

As a customer using the mobile app, I want to cancel an order from its own
screen, after confirming, so that I cancel the order I meant to and only when I
mean to.

## Context

Today the app's My orders list offers Cancel on the list, with a confirmation
dialog. The issue moves it:

> "Cancel moves from the list into the order screen, at the bottom, as a text
> button. Keep the confirmation dialog." — LIN-GTB-3

The issue doesn't say which orders can be cancelled. Decided by default
(DEC-0004), the client may still change it: Cancel is offered for placed orders
only, not for shipped or cancelled ones. Q-0002 asks the client to confirm.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1 — No Cancel on the list

- **Given** a customer with orders of any status
- **When** they open My orders
- **Then** no card and no list row offers Cancel

### AC-2 — Cancel on the order screen

- **Given** a placed order
- **When** the customer opens its screen
- **Then** a Cancel text button is shown at the bottom of the screen, below the
  order's lines (DEC-0004)

### AC-3 — Not offered for other statuses

- **Given** a shipped or a cancelled order
- **When** the customer opens its screen
- **Then** no Cancel button is shown (DEC-0004)

### AC-4 — Confirmation kept

- **Given** a placed order's screen
- **When** the customer taps Cancel
- **Then** the confirmation dialog the list showed until now opens, and the
  order is not cancelled until the customer confirms

### AC-5 — Confirmed

- **Given** the confirmation dialog for a placed order
- **When** the customer confirms
- **Then** the order is cancelled, its screen shows the grey cancelled chip and
  no Cancel button, and back on My orders its card shows the cancelled chip

### AC-6 — Dismissed

- **Given** the confirmation dialog for a placed order
- **When** the customer dismisses it
- **Then** the order stays placed and its screen is unchanged

## Out of scope

- Any change to how the orders-service cancels an order.
- New wording of the confirmation dialog; it is kept as it is.
